📈 Key Market Trends (2025)

🥤 Gut Health & Functional Beverages Boom
PepsiCo's $1.95B acquisition of Poppi signals major CPG interest in prebiotic sodas and functional drinks. This category seeing highest multiples.
💪 Protein Innovation Beyond Bars
Companies expanding protein into new formats. Celsius acquired Alani Nu for female-focused wellness drinks and supplements.
🌱 Clean Label & Health-Conscious
Flowers Foods acquired Simple Mills (gluten-free). Better-for-you category accounting for majority of deal flow.
📦 Portfolio Repositioning
Large FMCG divesting non-core assets. Strategic buyers dominating (76% of H1 deals). Creates PE opportunities.
🌍 Global Cuisine Expansion
Hispanic foods and international flavors seeing increased M&A interest as consumers seek authentic, diverse options.
⚖️ Regulatory Reshaping
FD&C Red No. 3 ban by 2027 forcing reformulation. Tariff impacts on 17% of US food supply creating cost pressures.
